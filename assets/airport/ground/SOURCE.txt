Low-altitude cinematic ground image
==================================

File: lancaster_naip_2022.jpg, 4000 x 4000 pixels, 3,846,533 bytes.
Source: USDA National Agriculture Imagery Program, served by USGS The National Map.
Area: Lancaster County, Pennsylvania, USA; requested bounds
76.31 W, 39.95 N to 76.23 W, 40.01 N (server adjusts the square export extent).
Acquisition: six USDA 2022 source rasters, originally 0.6 m ground sample distance.
Export: natural-color JPEG at approximately 1.706 ground metres per pixel,
covering about 6.824 km per side at the central latitude. Web Mercator export
coordinates and individual raster acquisition metadata are in source.json.

Rights: US public domain. The official USDA catalog declares this dataset public
domain; the USGS service description explicitly describes its CONUS NAIP downloads
as public-domain orthoimagery. No commercial Esri/Google basemap tiles are used.
Credit: USGS, USDA, The National Map.

Official source service:
https://imagery.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/USGSNAIPImagery/ImageServer
Official license/dataset record:
https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/national-agriculture-imagery-program-naip-imagery

This image is a cinematic scenery insert for the fictional flight. It repeats
with mirrored edges in the ground shader; it is not a navigational map and is
not high-resolution imagery of the Mediterranean shown by the separate orbital
Earth view. The fictional runway is drawn by the shader over the real image.

The bundled JPEG runs completely offline. Reproduction and SHA256 verification:
python tools/fetch_airport_ground.py
