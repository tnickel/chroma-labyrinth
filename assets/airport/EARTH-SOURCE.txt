earth_mediterranean_500m.jpg
NASA EOSDIS Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS), BlueMarble_NextGeneration.
Static Blue Marble MODIS basemap at the published 500-m tile-matrix resolution.
Georeferenced region: 4.5 degrees W to 31.5 degrees E, 18 to 54 degrees N.
Image: 8192 x 8192, 0.00439453125 degrees/pixel (489.197 m/pixel at equator;
longitude ground spacing contracts with latitude). This is approximately
16 times the global fallback's linear detail, not an upscaled 5400 image.

The source uses native level-7/500m GIBS grid alignment. Four adjacent 4096-square
WMS requests assemble the region without resampling, added detail or colour edits.
The result is encoded as JPEG quality95, 4:4:4. Source download total: 6,291,839
bytes; shipped regional JPEG: 12,072,381 bytes. Godot imports BC7 with mipmaps.
