The pure-sky HDRI is by Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest.
sources.json records the original download URLs and verified MD5 checksums.
tools/fetch_airport_assets.ps1 reproduces the bounded asset download.
Maps are original 1K/2K albedo, OpenGL normals, roughness; sky is 2K HDR.
In-engine material tints and physical mapping scales are set in AirportMaterials.gd.

apron_concrete/: Concrete Tiles 02 by Charlotte Baglioni, Poly Haven CC0.
Original 2K JPEG diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps, not recoloured.
Exact download URLs and verified MD5 checksums are appended in sources.json.
Photographic joints map to staggered 2.5 x 5 m apron panels; other concrete
materials keep their original source and scale. Texture imports use mipmaps.
