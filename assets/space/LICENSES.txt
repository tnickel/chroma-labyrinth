CHROMA LUNAR FLIGHT - IMAGE AND ELEVATION CREDITS

Textures in this directory derive from the NASA SVS, NASA Earth Observatory,
LROC Planetary Data System and USGS products listed below. They are shipped locally; the
game never downloads data. SHA256, exact source URLs, coordinates, dimensions
and processing are recorded in SOURCE.json. Conversion is reproducible with
tools/fetch_space_assets.py. Conversion dependencies are build tools only.

1. Global lunar color and LOLA elevations
   Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio / Ernie Wright.
   Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter / LROC camera team and LOLA instrument team.
   CGI Moon Kit, https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
   Color source: 2019 LROC 16384x8192 RGB map (Hapke normalized WAC mosaic).
   Elevation source: LOLA 64 pixels/degree unsigned half-metre global map.
   Public domain under the NASA SVS usage statement:
   https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/ (Can I use your visualizations?)

3. Star sky
   Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center Scientific Visualization Studio.
   Gaia DR2: ESA/Gaia/DPAC. Visualization: Ernie Wright (USRA).
   Deep Star Maps 2020, https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851/
   Credits checked against the official source page on 2026-10-30.
   Only the star map is used; constellation figure/grid overlays are not bundled.
   Catalog-derived sky using Hipparcos-2, Tycho-2 and Gaia DR2 with supporting
   catalogs described on the source page. The Milky Way is formed from those
   measured star distributions; this is not a randomly generated star texture.
   Public domain under https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/help/.
   Original 8192x4096 linear OpenEXR (all values within [0,1]) converted to
   sRGB8 lossless PNG without resizing, clipping or tonemapping. This conversion
   introduces finite 8-bit quantization, documented in SOURCE.json.
   No NASA logo or endorsement is implied.

SCIENTIFIC LIMITS
The global color map was prepared by NASA for visualization. The regional
photograph contains baked sunlight and shadows. The 5 m stereo DEM covers a
narrower strip than the orthophoto; surrounding heights use the coarser LOLA
measurements. Resampling/feathering makes their join usable for graphics,
but does not create additional measured detail. These graphics are not
scientific navigation data or a precise reconstruction of current illumination.
