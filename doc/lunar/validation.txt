CHROMA LUNAR - VALIDATION, 2026-10-30

Latest update: landing, walkable base and lunar surface
- The itinerary now has 21 phases and takes 847 seconds / 14:07, excluding
  the optional interior visit. It includes lunar descent, a 45-second stop
  on LandingPad2, and ascent. B opens the interior only on the ground. At
  either marked airlock, E switches between the interior and walkable surface;
  the surface HUD shows the direction and distance back. B or Escape returns
  from either stage to the cabin. The entire visit pauses the flight clock.
  Cabin, interior and surface are separate stages with transitions, not a
  continuous walk from the ship to the habitat. During the surface visit an
  Apollo-based scenic lander stands in for the aircraft's outer appearance.

  LunarSurfaceWalkRegression: 72 checks; LunarBaseWalkabilityRegression:
  51 checks; LunarExteriorGeometryRegression: 41 checks. All passed. These
  include actual player movement, airlock inputs, jump and return, state
  restoration, lighting layers, pad geometry and the observatory glazing.
  LunarExteriorBoundaryRegression: 512 physics rays with no failure; the
  boundary covers the measured terrain heights and the allowed jump.

Production journey
- 19 phases, 770 seconds / 12:50. Moon approach, 150-second Copernicus
  survey, three photographed lunar-base phases, Earthrise, return to Earth
  and boarding at the original gate.
- Closest altitude is 5 km above the lunar reference sphere (1737.4 km
  radius) during the crater sweep and 1.6 km during the base close pass,
  not a terrain-following clearance of exactly those figures.
- Scenic time compression and staged viewing attitude; no orbital mechanics
  or current astronomical constellation is claimed.

Photographed lunar settlement
- scripts/airport/LunarBase.gd places a real 3D compound at 10.324N/18.484W
  inside the NAC strip: the flattest measured kilometre along the ground
  track (24 m relief over the 1.1 km graded pad, LOLA/NAC stereo).
- Placement mirrors the sky shader exactly: stage_origin +
  moon_direction*(R+altitude) + world_to_geo^T*geo_dir*(R+site_elevation);
  the site elevation is decoded on the CPU from moon_region_height.png.
- Visible below 24 km altitude with lunar_mix > 0.55; the flight camera far
  plane is 30 km and the sky dome 26 km so the true-scale compound renders
  inside the depth-writing shell during its phases.
- Textures are photographic crops of flown hardware: ISS Solar Array Wing
  (s116e05970), JEM Kibo hull plating (s124e006729), Apollo 11 LM gold
  blanket and white ascent panels (as11-40-5927). Sources, crop rectangles
  and SHA256 in assets/space/SOURCE.json; no painted or generated detail.

Walkable interior (B key during the three base phases)
- scripts/airport/LunarBaseInterior.gd builds the settlement interior at an
  offset stage 80 m below the airfield: airlock with suit racks, spine
  corridor, mess hall (real CC0 lounge furniture), greenhouse with potted
  plants and magenta grow lights, laboratory, control room and an entrance
  ramp up to a 360-degree glass observation dome (ten-pane drum plus cone
  roof to a lit hub).
- Walls use the photographed Apollo white thermal panels; equipment uses the
  gold blanket; floors and worktops reuse the bundled CC0 PBR scans with
  world triplanar mapping. Ceiling light panels, emissive guide strips and
  omni fills light the rooms; the low lunar Sun throws real window patches.
- Windows and the dome look through the flight camera's own environment: the
  raymarched terrain, catalog star sky and Earth stay live. The visit holds
  the flight clock and hides the parked aircraft; B returns the visitor to
  the exact prior seat or standing spot and restores the resumed phase.
- LunarFlightRegression covers prompt visibility, teleport, held clock,
  blocked phase jumps/early return while inside, floor landing and exact
  return; lunar_review.gd captures mess, greenhouse and dome interiors.

Visual fixes verified on the GPU
- Grazing lunar rays converge to a solid horizon instead of missing terrain.
- Wide photographic coverage replaces the white coarse ring beyond the NAC
  strip. Exposure matching and geographic feathering blend image products.
- Transparent glass no longer paints diffuse structural shadows across sky
  or lunar terrain. Dielectric reflections and actual window frames remain.
- Distant Earth uses real cloud coverage instead of striped procedural noise;
  optical 10-degree diagnostic confirms readable continents and cloud systems.
- The Earth remains its physical angular size of approximately two degrees
  when viewed near the Moon; the 10-degree capture is explicitly a QA zoom.

Screenshots are unretouched Godot viewport captures. The photo's deep crater
